## What It Is

driveCREATE is a $10 digital bundle designed to take Oklahoma teen drivers from nervous passenger to confident driver. Study guides, defensive driving blueprints, parent co-pilot strategy, visual infographics, and a complete study workbook cover the full Oklahoma permit process through Service Oklahoma.

Built for families doing Parent-Taught Driver Education (PTDE) through Service Oklahoma. Verified against Service Oklahoma requirements, July 2026.

---

## What's Inside the Bundle

- **Oklahoma Driver Blueprint**: every requirement, fee, and GDL deadline in order
- **Defensive Driving Blueprint**: 3-second rule, blind spot elimination, hydroplaning recovery
- **Mindset Upgrade System**: box breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, cortisol management
- **Parent Co-Pilot Strategy**: empty lot drills, commentary driving, the no-raised-voices rule
- **15+ Visual Infographics**: GDL timeline, signs decoder, right-of-way, danger zones (printable)
- **Complete Study Workbook**: condensed, practical, built for real life

## Key Facts (verified July 2026)

- Learner Permit at 15½ with Driver Ed enrolled or finished, or 16 without
- Written Exam: 20 questions, 15 to pass, $4 per failed retake
- Permit issuance fee: $42.50
- Hold the permit 180 days, log 50 practice hours, 10 at night
- Work Zone Safe course required before the Intermediate License
- PTDE does not waive the Written Exam

## Updating the photos

The page has three real-photo slots. To swap a photo, replace the file in `assets/photos/` and keep the exact name. If a file is missing, an elegant "Photo coming soon" frame shows instead.

- `angie-tory-car.jpg` · Tory driving with Angie as co-pilot (top of the page)
- `angie-tory-night.jpg` · Angie and Tory by the car at night (wide photo above the story)
- `angie-office.jpg` · Angie in the driveCREATE office ("Why this exists" section)

Portrait or square photos look best. Around 1200 pixels wide is plenty.
